FerroVault Privacy Policy

Effective 12 June 2026

FerroVault does not collect, transmit, or store any of your data on our servers — we don't operate any servers. Your photos and videos travel exclusively between your device and the FerroVault server you run on your own computer, over your own local network.

No analytics, no tracking, no third-party services, no accounts with us. Data the app stores on your device (login session for your own server) never leaves your control.
