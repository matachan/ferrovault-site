Getting started

FerroVault is two pieces: the iOS app and the FerroVault Server you run on your own computer, NAS, or home server. Install the server first (a single file, no complicated setup), then point the app at it — type the address, or scan the QR code shown on the server's dashboard. From there, back up your library at full resolution and restore anything back to your device whenever you want.

Common questions

The app lost access to my photos after an iOS update

Restart your iPhone first. This is an occasional iOS glitch, not a FerroVault setting — a reboot almost always restores access. Reinstalling the app is the last resort, not the first.

Does FerroVault back up in the background?

Yes. With Background Sync enabled, iOS periodically uploads new photos on its own — most reliably while your iPhone is charging, locked, and on WiFi (that's how iOS schedules background work for every app). There's no progress bar because the system does the transfer itself. Opening the app always syncs everything immediately, and WiFi-Only applies to background uploads too.

Why don't Live Photos back up in the background?

A Live Photo is two files — the still and a short motion video — that only count as backed up together. Apple's background-upload system sends files one at a time with no guarantee the pair stays together, so FerroVault refuses to risk archiving a Live Photo as an ordinary still. Your Live Photos back up — both parts, properly paired — every time you open the app. Nothing is skipped; they just wait for the app to do it right.

Do edited photos survive a restore?

Yes. FerroVault backs up your edited photo and the untouched original. A restored photo comes back exactly as you edited it, and Edit → Revert to Original in the Photos app recovers the unedited version. One honest limitation that applies to every non-Apple app: the individual edit settings (exposure sliders, crop values) can't be re-opened for fine-tuning on a restored photo — new edits apply on top of the edited look, and the original is always preserved.

Can I revert an edited Live Photo after restoring it?

Not yet. A restored edited Live Photo keeps its edited look and still plays as a Live Photo, but Revert to Original isn't available on it — that needs server-side support we're building. Regular (non-Live) edited photos revert normally.

Is it safe to use Free Up Space?

Yes — that's the point of it. Free Up Space verifies your backup byte-for-byte before anything leaves your phone, and removing something from the server puts it in a recoverable trash, never a void.

Deleting your account

Settings → Delete Account removes your account and sign-in from your server (password-confirmed). Your backed-up photos and videos are not deleted — they stay on the server's drive and can only be erased at the server itself, by design.
